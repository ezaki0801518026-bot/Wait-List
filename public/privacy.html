Privacy notice for the WA-Chain Edu waitlist

Version of 5 October 2026. This notice covers the waitlist and matches the WA-Chain Privacy Policy (version 1.0, 2 October 2026).

Who we are

WA-Chain, a student-led team based in Sapporo, Hokkaido, Japan. We will give our full address promptly on request.
Contact: ezaki.yoshifumi.i1@elms.hokudai.ac.jp, or reply to your confirmation email.

What we collect

  • Your name, country or region, type of affiliation, and email address
  • The language you used, whether you asked for WA-Chain updates, and the date and time you registered
  • The version of this notice you agreed to

To stop automated sign-ups, the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks signals from your browser. We do not receive those signals. We do not use analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

Why we use it

We use your details only to provide WA-Chain Edu to you in the way that suits you best. In practice, this means:

  • confirming your registration and telling you your place on the waitlist
  • telling you about the release and how to access WA-Chain Edu
  • replying when you contact us
  • understanding, as overall numbers only, which countries and types of organisation need the service, so that we can shape it for you

Only if you tick the optional box, we also send you occasional WA-Chain updates. You can stop them at any time.

We never sell your details or share them with anyone for their own purposes.

Where your details go

Your details pass through two companies only. Both are in the United States.

CompanyRoleTheir privacy policy
Cloudflare, Inc. (USA)Hosts this page, carries your submission to Google over an encrypted connection without storing it, and runs the bot checkcloudflare.com/privacypolicy
Google LLC (USA)Stores the waitlist in a private Google Sheet in WA-Chain’s account and sends your confirmation email through Gmailpolicies.google.com/privacy

Both companies act on our behalf. For the data protection system in the United States, see the summary published by Japan’s Personal Information Protection Commission: ppc.go.jp (United States). When you tick the consent box, you agree to your details being handled by these companies in the United States.

How long we keep it

  • Until 1 November 2027 (one year after the planned release), or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first
  • If you asked for updates: until you unsubscribe

After that, we delete your details. We may keep overall numbers that cannot identify you, such as how many people registered from each country.

How we protect it

  • Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS). Your details are never put in web addresses or server logs.
  • The spreadsheet is not shared with anyone. Only the team members who run the waitlist can open it, and the account uses two-step verification.
  • The link between this page and the spreadsheet is protected by a secret key that never reaches your browser.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your details, to stop using them, or to stop sending emails. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Reply to your confirmation email or write to the contact above. There is no charge. If you live in the EEA or the UK, the legal basis is your consent, and you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Under 16

If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before you register.
